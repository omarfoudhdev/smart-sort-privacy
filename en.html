In short: Smart Sort has no account and no server of its own. Your game progress stays on your phone. Ads, statistics, crash reports and purchases are handled by Google services, under your consent choices.

1. Who we are

Smart Sort (Android package com.fddev.smartsort) is published by FDDev.

For any question or request about your data: omar.foudh.dev@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

2. Data stored only on your device

The game saves on your phone: your levels and stars, coins, lives and boosters, your settings (sound, music, vibration, colour-blind mode), which purchases you own, and a random installation identifier generated by the app.

This data has no name, email or account attached. Android backup is disabled for this app: uninstalling it deletes everything.

3. Google services used by the game

  • Google AdMob (ads) and the Google consent form (UMP): the advertising ID, device information (model, OS version, language), IP address (used for approximate location) and ad interactions, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the consent form is shown before any ad request; ads are personalised only if you accept.
  • Firebase Analytics: in-game events (levels started and finished, boosters used, rewards, purchases, ads shown), the random installation identifier and device information, to understand difficulty and improve the game. Where consent is required, analytics storage stays off until you accept.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash and error reports (device state, app version, the random installation identifier), to fix bugs.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: a technical device token, to deliver game notifications (for example when your lives are full again).
  • Google Play Billing: purchases are processed by Google Play. We only receive the purchased product and its confirmation, never your payment details.

These services act under Google's own rules: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from apps that use its services · Firebase privacy.

4. Why we use your data (legal bases)

To run the game and deliver your purchases (performance of the contract); to show ads that fund the free game and to measure its use (your consent where required, otherwise our legitimate interest); to keep the game stable and secure (legitimate interest).

5. How long data is kept

Data on your device: until you uninstall the game. Statistics in Google Analytics: at most 14 months. Crash reports: 90 days. Purchase records are kept by Google Play under its own rules.

6. Your choices and your rights

  • Ads consent: change it at any time in the game: ⚙ → padlock button « Privacy options » (shown where the law requires it).
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your Android settings (Google → Ads).
  • Notifications: turn them off in the Android settings of the app.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the game.

Depending on where you live (notably the GDPR in Europe), you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. Write to omar.foudh.dev@gmail.com; you may also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

7. Children

Smart Sort is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has used the game in a way that involves their data, contact us and we will help.

8. Security

Game data lives in the app's private storage, isolated by Android from other apps. Exchanges with Google services are encrypted (HTTPS).

9. Changes

We will update this page if the game changes how it handles data. The date at the top shows the current version.
